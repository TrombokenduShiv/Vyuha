About the Hackathon 
byteBuilt 1.0 is a 24-hour innovation hackathon where all college students collaborate to solve 
real-world problems by building creative and practical technology solutions. 
Participants will work in teams to identify a problem, design an innovative solution, build a 
prototype or proof of concept, and present it before an expert jury. 
 
Who Can Participate?

●​

○​
Minimum: 3 Members 
○​
Maximum: 4 Members 
●​
Teams can be self-formed. 
●​
Every team must choose a unique team name.

Hackathon Flow 
Phase 1 – Registration & Idea Submission 
●​
Register your team through the official registration portal. 
●​
Submit the team details. 
●​
Submit a Pitch Deck (PPT) explaining your idea. 
●​
Each team may submit only one idea. 
●​
Ensure that the idea is original and aligns with the hackathon themes. 
●​
More than 100 teams are expected to participate.
 
Phase 2 – Shortlisting 
Based on the submitted Pitch Decks, the Top 60 teams will be shortlisted for the next round.
 
Phase 3 – Project Video Submission 
The shortlisted teams must submit a project presentation video demonstrating their             
proposed solution. 
Video Specifications 
●​
Duration: Minimum 2 minutes and  maximum 4 minutes. 
●​
Format: MP4 
●​
Resolution: 1080p (Full HD) preferred (Minimum: 720p HD) 
●​
Orientation: Landscape (16:9) 
●​
Frame Rate: Minimum 24 FPS 
●​
Audio: Clear and audible with minimal background noise. 
●​
Language: English (preferred) or English with subtitles if required. 
 
 
                                                                                                                              Organized by                                          & 
 

 
Note: Please upload an unlisted youtube link in google form provided after the selection process. 
Video Quality Guidelines 
●​
Ensure the video is well-lit and clearly visible. 
●​
Use a stable camera or tripod to avoid shaky footage. 
●​
Record in a quiet environment with clear audio. 
●​
Screen recordings should be high resolution so that the prototype and UI are easy to read. 
●​
Avoid excessive background music or distracting visual effects. 
●​
Ensure all team members are clearly visible and audible while presenting their respective 
contributions. 
●​
Verify that the video plays correctly before submission. 
The Project Video Should Cover: 
1.​
Team Introduction 
i.​
Team name 
ii.​ Member introductions 
iii.​ Individual roles and responsibilities 
2.​
Problem Statement 
i.​
What problem are you solving? 
ii.​ Why is this problem important? 
3.​
Proposed Solution 
i.​
Explain your idea clearly. 
ii.​ Highlight the uniqueness and innovation. 
4.​
Technology Stack 
i.​
Tools, programming languages, frameworks, APIs, or AI tools used. 
5.​
Expected Impact 
i.​
Who will benefit from your solution? 
ii.​ What real-world impact can it create? 
6.​
Future Scope 
i.​
Possible enhancements and scalability of the project. 
Based on the project videos, the Top 40 teams will be selected for the Grand Finale(In Person) at 
Chandigarh University, Mohali. 
 
Phase 4 – Grand Finale (24 Hours) 
The shortlisted teams will participate in a 24-hour hackathon, where they will: 
●​
Build and refine their prototype. 
●​
Enhance features and functionality.

●​
Receive mentorship from industry experts. 
●​
Prepare the final project demonstration and presentation. 
 
 
Phase 5 – Final Presentation 
Each team will present their solution before the jury. 
Presentation Format 
Presentation and Jury Q&A : Maximum 7 minutes for presentation and 3 minutes for Q&A. 
The jury will evaluate the projects based on innovation, impact, feasibility, prototype quality, and 
presentation before announcing the winners.
